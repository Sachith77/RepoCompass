# Cutoff validation

Generated by `python -m repocompass validate --months 24 optuna/optuna sqlfluff/sqlfluff pydantic/pydantic fastapi/fastapi`. All values are measured from the stored analyses.

## Method (fixed before running)

- Window: each analysis covers 730 days (24 months) of PRs. Cutoff: 365 days before each repository's snapshot.
- Before sample: newcomer PRs created at least 30 days before the cutoff and merged or closed by the cutoff, judged from `merged_at` / `closed_at` rather than from their state today.
- After sample: newcomer PRs created on or after the cutoff and at least 30 days before the snapshot, merged or closed by the snapshot.
- Bands are made on the before sample exactly as in a report: the 95% Wilson interval of an area's merge rate against the before repo rate.
- An area **held** if its after merge rate was on the same side of the after repo rate (above for "above repo average", below for "below repo average").
- Only areas with at least 10 mature newcomer PRs in both periods count.
- Bootstrap: 10,000 resamples of the qualifying areas, seed 0, percentile interval.

## Limitations

- Newcomer status comes from the stored analysis, which used merges up to the snapshot, so some before-cutoff PRs may be classified with information from after the cutoff (each repository below gives an upper bound).
- Only the last close or merge time of a PR is stored: a PR closed before the cutoff and reopened after it looks open at the cutoff and drops out of the before sample.
- The bootstrap treats areas as independent, though areas of one repository share tied PRs and are compared with a repo rate that includes their own PRs.
- Areas are those of the tree at the snapshot; PRs keep the area they were assigned then.
- The before period holds about 11 months of PRs and the after period about 11, so areas can fall below the minimum sample in one of them. One cutoff per repository.
- A sampled analysis (any month capped) validates the sample, not every PR.
