# Cutoff validation: what it shows

Two runs of the same check, each generated by `python -m repocompass validate` on 2026-11-04 from
stored analyses. This page only reads them; the tables, method and limitations are in each
`summary.md`, the per-area numbers in the JSON files next to it.

| mode | repositories | cutoff | files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 months | Kludex/starlette, optuna/optuna, sqlfluff/sqlfluff | 182 days before the snapshot | `summary.md` here |
| 24 months | optuna/optuna, sqlfluff/sqlfluff, pydantic/pydantic, fastapi/fastapi | 365 days before the snapshot | `window24/` |

The question (docs/BRIEF.md): are areas banded "above repo average" before a cutoff still above the repo
rate after it? An area "held" if its after merge rate was on the same side of the after repo rate. Only
areas with at least 10 mature newcomer PRs in both periods count.

## Side by side

| | 12 months | 24 months |
|---|---|---|
| areas with at least 10 before the cutoff | 7 (2 + 4 + 1) | 14 (0 + 2 + 3 + 9) |
| of those, also at least 10 after | 4 | 11 |
| "above repo average": held / qualifying | 0 / 0, not computable | 1 / 1 (pydantic `docs`) |
| "below repo average": held / qualifying | 2 / 2 (optuna/study, sqlfluff `src`) | 1 / 1 (pydantic `pydantic`) |
| "no clear difference": above the after repo rate | 0 / 2 | 5 / 9 |

## The answer: still too few areas to say

- **More areas qualified in the 24-month run (11 against 4), but mostly because it added two
  repositories**: 9 of the 11 come from pydantic (3) and fastapi (6). On optuna and sqlfluff, which are
  in both runs, 2 areas qualified each time (optuna 1 → 0, sqlfluff 1 → 2). Neither 12-month directional
  band was directional again in the 24-month before period: `optuna/study` had too few PRs there, and
  sqlfluff `src` was "no clear difference" (0.903 against a before repo rate of 0.920). So the effect of
  the longer window alone was not separated from the effect of adding repositories.
- **Every directional band that could be tested held**: 2 of 2 in the 12-month run, 1 of 1 for each
  band in the 24-month run. That is 4 areas in all. With so few, each bootstrap interval is
  degenerate ([1.000, 1.000]) and carries no information, and the two runs cannot be pooled: they share
  optuna and sqlfluff and overlap in time.
- **The 24-month "above" area**: pydantic `docs`, 0.733 before (30 PRs, interval [0.556, 0.858]) against
  a before repo rate of 0.483, and 0.493 after (67 PRs) against an after repo rate of 0.223.
- **The 24-month "below" area**: the `pydantic` package, 0.366 before (71 PRs) and 0.157 after (287 PRs)
  against 0.223.
- In the 12-month run: `optuna/study` 0.100 before and 0.167 after against 0.319; sqlfluff `src` 0.571
  before and 0.460 after against 0.524. sqlfluff's `src` is the whole package (its area rule only splits
  `test`), so that result says little about any one part of the code.

This remains a null result: the bands are not shown to predict anything, and they are not shown to fail.

## What changed between the periods

The repo rate dropped from the earlier period to the later one in every repository of the 24-month run:
optuna 0.792 → 0.461, sqlfluff 0.920 → 0.567, pydantic 0.483 → 0.223, fastapi 0.275 → 0.054 (measured).
In the 12-month run it rose for starlette (0.132 → 0.234) and fell for optuna (0.540 → 0.319) and
sqlfluff (0.750 → 0.524). This is why each area is compared with the repo rate of its own period. The
cause of the drops was not checked.

The earlier period also held fewer mature newcomer PRs in most repositories: 12 months, starlette 38
against 145 and sqlfluff 56 against 212 (optuna the reverse, 87 against 47); 24 months, optuna 48 against
141, sqlfluff 75 against 289, pydantic 118 against 453, fastapi 273 against 699.

## How much could leak from after the cutoff

Newcomer status comes from the stored analysis, which knew about merges up to the snapshot. Each
summary gives an upper bound per repository on before-cutoff PRs stored as non-newcomer with no earlier
PR by the same author in the stored window that merged before the cutoff:

- 12 months: at most 3 (starlette), 13 (optuna), 21 (sqlfluff);
- 24 months: at most 15 (optuna), 22 (sqlfluff), 30 (pydantic), 32 (fastapi).

These are upper bounds, not counts: an author's earlier merged PR may simply be older than the stored
window. They are not small next to the thinner before samples (optuna 24 months: at most 15 against 48),
so the before samples could be distorted by up to that much. Reopened PRs and areas taken from the
snapshot tree are further limitations, listed in each `summary.md`.

## What would make it informative

More repositories with several directional bands each, or repeating the check at several cutoffs, so
that dozens of "above" and "below" areas qualify rather than one or two. In these runs the extra
qualifying areas came mainly from the added repositories, and they were mostly "no clear difference";
the longer window's own effect was not separated. None of these was run; they are not results.
