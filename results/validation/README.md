# Cutoff validation: what it shows

The tables, the method and the limitations are in `summary.md`, generated by
`python -m repocompass validate Kludex/starlette optuna/optuna sqlfluff/sqlfluff` on 2026-11-04 from
the stored analyses (starlette and optuna: snapshot 2026-11-03; sqlfluff: snapshot 2026-11-04). The
per-area numbers are in the three JSON files, the pooled numbers in `pooled.json`. This page only reads
them.

## The question

The brief asks: are areas banded "above repo average" before a cutoff still above the repo rate after
it? Cutoff: 182 days before each snapshot.

## The answer: this data cannot say

- **Above repo average: not computable.** No area was banded "above repo average" before the cutoff
  and had at least 10 mature newcomer PRs in both periods, in any of the three repositories. So the
  report's headline band was never tested.
- **Below repo average: 2 of 2 held** (`optuna/study`, 0.100 before and 0.167 after against an after
  repo rate of 0.319; sqlfluff `src`, 0.571 before and 0.460 after against 0.524). The bootstrap 95% CI
  of [1.000, 1.000] is degenerate: resampling two values that are both "held" can only give 1.0. It
  says both areas held, not that the band is reliable.
- **For comparison:** the two "no clear difference" areas that qualified (starlette's `starlette` and
  `tests`) both ended below the after repo rate (0 of 2 above).

Two areas in total is far too few for a conclusion either way. This is a null result, reported as one.
Note also that sqlfluff's `src` is the whole package (its area rule only split `test`), so its result
says little about any one part of the code.

## Why so few areas qualified

- Only 7 areas had at least 10 mature newcomer PRs before the cutoff (2 + 4 + 1), and 4 of them also
  had at least 10 after it.
- Each analysis covers 12 months, so each period holds about five months of PRs, and the minimum of 10
  applies to each period separately.
- For starlette and sqlfluff the earlier period is the thinner one: 38 mature newcomer PRs before the
  cutoff against 145 after it, and 56 against 212. PR volume rose through their year. Optuna is the
  reverse, 87 against 47; its cause was not checked.
- The repo rate itself moved between the periods (starlette 0.132 → 0.234, optuna 0.540 → 0.319,
  sqlfluff 0.750 → 0.524), which is why each area is compared with the repo rate of its own period.

## How much could leak from after the cutoff

Newcomer status comes from the stored analysis, which knew about merges up to the snapshot.
`summary.md` gives an upper bound per repository: at most 3 (starlette), 13 (optuna) and 21 (sqlfluff)
before-cutoff PRs are stored as non-newcomer with no earlier PR by the same author in the stored 12
months that merged before the cutoff. These are upper bounds, not counts: the author's earlier merged
PR may simply be older than the stored window. Reopened PRs and areas taken from the snapshot tree are
listed as limitations in `summary.md`.

## What would make it informative

A longer history (several years of PRs rather than one), more repositories, or repeating the check at
several cutoffs. Each would raise the number of areas that qualify in both periods. None of these was
run; they are not results.
